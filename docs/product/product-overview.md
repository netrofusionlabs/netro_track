## 2. What is NetroTrack?

NetroTrack is a cloud-based, mobile-first Field Workforce Management Platform that enables organizations to digitize and manage their complete field workforce from a single application.

The platform provides real-time visibility into:

- Employee attendance
- Live GPS tracking
- Customer visits
- Field inspections
- Product sales
- Employee productivity
- Business operations

Instead of using multiple disconnected applications for attendance, tracking, visits, reporting, and inspections, NetroTrack consolidates everything into **one unified platform**.

## 5. Product Objectives

NetroTrack aims to replace multiple disconnected systems with one integrated platform:

| # | Objective | Current Problem |
|---|-----------|----------------|
| 1 | Digitize employee attendance | Manual or paper-based attendance cannot be verified |
| 2 | Provide real-time employee location tracking | Managers have no visibility into field employee locations |
| 3 | Verify customer visits using GPS and images | Customer visits cannot be validated |
| 4 | Record field inspections | Inspections recorded on paper — delayed, unreliable |
| 5 | Record product sales and orders | Sales data is delayed and fragmented |
| 6 | Provide management dashboards | Managers lack real-time operational visibility |
| 7 | Improve employee accountability | No mechanism to verify field activity |
| 8 | Generate meaningful reports | Manual reporting is slow and error-prone |
| 9 | Reduce paperwork | Field operations rely on physical forms |
| 10 | Improve decision-making through analytics | No centralized data for analysis |

---

## 6. Business Problem

Organizations with field employees face these operational challenges:

- Employees mark attendance but there is no verification mechanism.
- Managers do not know the real-time location of field employees.
- Customer visits are self-reported and cannot be validated.
- Reporting is manual, delayed, and error-prone.
- Sales data arrives days after the transaction.
- Field inspections are recorded on paper and often lost.
- Multiple disconnected applications are used for different activities.
- Management lacks real-time visibility into operations.

---

NetroTrack addresses these challenges with:

|---------|---------------|

## 9. Product Type

NetroTrack is a **commercial SaaS platform**:

- **One platform** serves **multiple companies** (multi-tenant).
- Each company (tenant) has complete data isolation.
- Each company has its own employees, managers, reports, and data.
- Future: company-specific branding (white-label), subscription tiers.

---

## 10. Product Philosophy

NetroTrack must always be:

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|------------|
| **Mobile First** | Built primarily for mobile use by field employees |
| **Simple** | Minimal learning curve — usable from day one |
| **Fast** | Sub-3-second launch, instant interactions |
| **Enterprise Grade** | Built for commercial deployment, not prototyping |
| **Secure** | End-to-end security, tenant isolation |
| **Scalable** | Designed for 500K+ employees |
| **Offline Capable** | Works in areas with no connectivity |
| **Battery Optimized** | GPS tracking that doesn't kill the battery |
| **Easy to Learn** | No training manual should be required |
| **Easy to Deploy** | New companies onboarded in minutes |

## 11. Success Criteria

NetroTrack is successful when:

1. Employees can complete their entire daily workflow using only the mobile app.
2. Managers have real-time visibility into field operations.
3. Client admins can manage their workforce without external tools.
4. Super admins can onboard and manage multiple companies.
5. The platform scales to thousands of organizations and hundreds of thousands of users.
